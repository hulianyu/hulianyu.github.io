Interpretable Clustering Repository

A curated repository of literature on interpretable and explainable clustering methods, structured around our comprehensive survey published in ACM Computing Surveys (2026). Covers in-clustering and post-clustering interpretability, ranging from decision-tree methods to rule-based, prototype-based, and many other interpretable and explainable clustering models worth exploring and applying.
